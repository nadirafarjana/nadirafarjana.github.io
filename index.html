Research focus

01

Computational materials

Investigation of emerging semiconductor materials using first-principles calculations, with emphasis on structural stability, electronic properties, band structure, density of states and optical behaviour.

02

Photovoltaic devices

Modelling and optimization of photovoltaic absorber materials and device architectures, including thickness, doping, defect and interface engineering for improved device performance.

03

AI & intelligent systems

Development of machine-learning and computer-vision approaches for practical engineering applications, including object detection, assistive technologies and intelligent embedded systems.

04

Sustainable engineering

Engineering solutions involving renewable energy, solar tracking, environmental monitoring, biogas systems and intelligent agricultural technologies.

02

Current research

My current work focuses on computational investigation of semiconductor materials and their application to next-generation photovoltaic technologies.

I use density functional theory and device-level simulation to investigate material properties and photovoltaic behaviour, while also exploring machine-learning-assisted analysis and optimization.

Alongside computational research, I continue to work on practical intelligent systems involving computer vision, embedded platforms, automation and sustainable energy technologies.
