02 / FEATURED RESEARCH

Undergraduate Thesis

First-Principles Investigation of Composition-Driven Properties in Mixed-Halide Mg₃PCl₃₋ₓBrₓ Materials

This research investigates the composition-dependent structural, electronic, optical, mechanical, thermal, and phonon properties of Mg₃PCl₃₋ₓBrₓ materials for x = 0, 1, 2, and 3 using first-principles calculations.
