01 About Me

I am an Electrical and Electronic Engineering graduate from Jamalpur Science & Technology University. My work focuses closely on computational materials science and device-level modeling, combining first-principles computations to explore high-efficiency energy materials.

I operate at the intersection of Density Functional Theory (DFT), renewable energy designs, and machine learning structures to model future optoelectronic systems.
