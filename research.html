Current Research · Undergraduate Thesis

First-Principles Investigation of Composition-Driven Structural, Electronic, Optical, Mechanical, and Thermal Properties in Mixed-Halide Mg₃PCl₃₋ₓBrₓ Materials

x = 0, 1, 2, and 3

This research investigates the composition-dependent properties of mixed-halide Mg₃PCl₃₋ₓBrₓ materials using first-principles density functional theory calculations. The study aims to understand how halide substitution influences the structural, electronic, optical, mechanical, thermal, and vibrational characteristics of these semiconductor materials.

Research Objectives

  • Analyze composition-dependent structural properties.
  • Investigate electronic band structures and density of states.
  • Evaluate optical properties and potential optoelectronic applications.
  • Study mechanical stability and elastic properties.
  • Investigate thermal and vibrational characteristics.
  • Analyze phonon dispersion and dynamical stability.

Photovoltaic Device Modeling

Perovskite Solar Cell Simulation and Performance Optimization

I have conducted numerical simulations of emerging photovoltaic materials and heterojunction solar-cell architectures using SCAPS-1D. My work involves evaluating absorber and charge-transport layers, optimizing device parameters, and analyzing photovoltaic performance.

Research Activities

  • Modeling single-junction and heterojunction solar cells.
  • Optimization of absorber and charge transport layers.
  • Analysis of photovoltaic parameters and device performance.
  • Investigation of emerging lead-free and halide-based materials.
  • Application of machine learning for performance analysis.
