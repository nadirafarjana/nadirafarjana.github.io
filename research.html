Research Statement

Research

My research interests lie at the intersection of computational materials science, photovoltaic device engineering, artificial intelligence and intelligent embedded systems.

01

Computational Materials & Photovoltaics

DFT

First-Principles Materials Modeling

Investigation of structural, electronic, mechanical and optical properties of semiconductor materials using density functional theory and Quantum ESPRESSO.

Device Modeling

Photovoltaic Device Simulation

SCAPS-1D modeling of perovskite solar cells, heterojunction architectures, charge transport layers, defect properties, doping concentration and absorber thickness.

Optimization

Machine Learning for Solar Cells

Application of machine learning to photovoltaic device datasets for performance prediction, parameter analysis and computational optimization.

02

Artificial Intelligence & Robotics

Vision

Computer Vision

Deep-learning-based object detection and recognition systems for practical engineering applications.

YOLO

Real-Time Object Detection

YOLO-based detection systems for currency recognition, assistive technologies and intelligent monitoring.

Robotics

Autonomous Intelligent Systems

Embedded computer vision, autonomous navigation, sensors, GPS and safety systems for robotics applications.

03

Renewable Energy & Sustainable Systems

Solar

Renewable Energy Systems

Design and analysis of photovoltaic systems and sustainable energy solutions.

IoT

Intelligent Monitoring

Sensor-based and IoT-enabled monitoring systems for energy, agriculture and environmental applications.
