Research directions

My work combines computational modelling, photovoltaic device analysis, machine learning and engineering implementation.

01

Computational materials science

First-principles investigation of emerging semiconductor materials, with emphasis on structural stability, electronic properties, band structure, density of states and optical response.

Methods Density Functional Theory
Quantum ESPRESSO
VESTA
Electronic-structure analysis
02

Photovoltaic materials & devices

Computational evaluation of absorber materials and photovoltaic device architectures, including thickness, doping, defect and interface optimization.

Methods SCAPS-1D
J–V analysis
QE / spectral response
Device optimization
03

Machine learning for engineering research

Application of machine learning and computer vision to scientific and engineering problems, including object detection, classification and data-driven analysis.

Methods Python
Machine Learning
YOLO
Computer Vision
04

Intelligent embedded systems

Development of embedded systems integrating sensors, microcontrollers, communication, automation and intelligent decision-making.

Technologies Arduino
Sensors
IoT
Embedded control
05

Sustainable energy systems

Engineering solutions involving photovoltaic generation, energy monitoring, off-grid systems, agricultural automation and resource-efficient technologies.

Focus Solar energy
Energy systems
Monitoring
Sustainable engineering

Research workflow

My computational research typically follows a material-to-device workflow: identify and validate a material, calculate its fundamental properties, interpret the electronic and optical behaviour, and evaluate its relevance at device level.
