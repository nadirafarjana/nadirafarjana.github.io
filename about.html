Research perspective

My research interests are centred on emerging semiconductor materials and their application to next-generation energy technologies.

I use computational approaches to investigate material properties and photovoltaic behaviour, while also exploring device-level modelling and optimization.

02

Engineering approach

Alongside computational research, I have developed practical engineering projects involving embedded systems, sensors, IoT, computer vision, robotics, solar energy and automation.

These projects have strengthened my ability to move from a system concept to hardware implementation and software-driven control.
